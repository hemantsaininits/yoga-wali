YOGA

The Essence of Yoga

Yoga is more than just physical exercise; it is a timeless journey of self-discovery and holistic healing. Rooted in ancient wisdom, our practice integrates breath, movement, and mindfulness to create a harmonious union of body, mind, and spirit.

At Ashika Jain Yoga Studio, we believe that yoga is for everyone. Whether you seek to build strength, increase flexibility, or simply find a moment of peace in a chaotic world, our diverse range of classes offers a path for every practitioner. Step onto the mat and reconnect with your inner self.

Types of Yoga We Offer

Hatha Yoga

A classical and grounding practice focusing on alignment, breath awareness, and holding postures mindfully. Ideal for building strength, flexibility, and inner stability.

Ashtanga Vinyasa Flow

A dynamic and energising flow that synchronises breath with movement. This practice builds stamina, discipline, focus, and physical endurance while calming the mind.

Prenatal Yoga

A safe and nurturing practice specially designed for expecting mothers. Focuses on gentle movement, breathwork, relaxation, and emotional support to prepare the body and mind for childbirth and motherhood.

Pranayama (Breathwork)

Conscious breathing techniques that regulate the nervous system, improve lung capacity, increase energy levels, and bring mental clarity. A powerful tool for stress management and inner balance.

Dhyana (Meditation)

Guided meditation and mindfulness practices to calm the mind, improve focus, and deepen self-awareness. Suitable for beginners as well as experienced practitioners.
