YOGA

About Yoga

Yoga is a sacred practice that unites body, breath, and awareness. Rooted in ancient wisdom, it guides us toward balance, strength, and inner peace.

At Ashika Jain Yoga Studio, yoga is approached as a holistic lifestyle — nurturing physical vitality while calming the mind and uplifting the spirit.

Types of Yoga

Hatha Yoga

Foundation Practice

A slow and grounding practice focusing on posture alignment, breath control, and mindful movement. Perfect for beginners and those seeking balance.

Ashtanga Yoga

Dynamic Flow

A powerful, disciplined sequence linking breath with movement. Builds strength, endurance, and deep mental focus.

Prenatal Yoga

Gentle & Nurturing

Designed for expecting mothers, focusing on gentle movement, breath awareness, relaxation, and emotional well-being.

Pranayama

Breathwork

Conscious breathing techniques that regulate energy, calm the nervous system, and enhance mental clarity.

Meditation

Inner Stillness

A mindful practice cultivating awareness, peace, and emotional balance. Suitable for beginners and experienced practitioners alike.
