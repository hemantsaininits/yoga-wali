About Dance

Dance is a universal language — a powerful expression of emotion, rhythm, and storytelling through movement. It connects body, mind, and soul.

At Ashika Jain Yoga Studio, dance is approached as both an art form and a therapeutic practice, helping you move with confidence, grace, and joy.

Dance Services We Offer

Wedding Choreography

Personalised Performances

Bespoke choreography for bride & groom, families, and friends. Perfect for sangeet, mehendi, and wedding celebrations — elegant, emotional, or energetic.

Group Choreography

All Ages & Skill Levels

Dynamic group routines designed for cultural events, stage performances, community gatherings, and school or college programs.

Corporate Event Choreography

High-Energy & Interactive

Professional performances and interactive dance sessions for corporate celebrations, team-building events, annual days, and offsites.

Classical & Fusion Training

Technique & Expression

A balanced blend of classical foundation and modern expression, focusing on posture, rhythm, storytelling, musicality, and grace.

Workshops & Special Projects

Short-Term & Creative

Themed workshops and creative collaborations for institutions, organisations, and private groups.
